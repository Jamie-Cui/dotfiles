* Document contract

This file is the source of truth for the paper's research content and workflow state.

Until the author explicitly approves the content and authorizes publication-format work:

1. Discuss and revise research content here.
2. Do not translate research changes into `.tex`, `.bib`, or LaTeX figures.
3. Mark uncertain claims as open.
4. Return substantive translation problems to this file.

Status markers:

- =[DECIDED]= author-approved direction
- =[PROPOSED]= current recommendation
- =[SUPPORTED]= evidence-backed claim or completed artifact
- =[OPEN]= unresolved claim, assumption, result, or choice
- =[OUT]= outside the main paper claim

* Content approval gates

** Gate A: contribution viability

- Prior art checked
- Delta and significance accepted

** Gate B: model and contract

- Roles, assumptions, guarantee, and non-goals accepted

** Gate C: construction and evidence

- Every central claim maps to an artifact, proof, or experiment
- Every central claim maps to an evaluation question or proof obligation

** Gate D: author approval

After explicit approval, set =content_approved= to =yes= and record =approved_at= and =approved_scope=.

Keep =latex_gate= frozen until the author explicitly authorizes LaTeX translation. Then set it to =authorized= and advance =paper_phase= to =latex-translation=.
